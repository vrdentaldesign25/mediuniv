Frequently Asked Questions

Is booking an appointment free for patients?
▼
Yes! Booking is completely free for patients. You only pay for the consultation itself, which goes to the healthcare provider.
Do I need to call the clinic after booking?
▼
Not at all! Your booking is confirmed instantly. You'll receive SMS and email confirmations, and the clinic has your appointment details automatically.
Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?
▼
Yes, you can reschedule or cancel up to 2 hours before your appointment through the app. Cancellation policies may vary by clinic.
What payment methods do you accept?
▼
We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, mobile wallets (Google Pay, Apple Pay), and UPI payments for maximum convenience.
Is my personal health information secure?
▼
Absolutely. We use hospital-grade encryption and comply with HIPAA standards. Your data is never shared without your explicit consent.
Can I book online consultations through MediBook?
▼
Yes! Many doctors on our platform offer online consultations via video call. You can filter by consultation type when searching.

Doctor Questions Answered

Do I need a dedicated receptionist to use MediBook?
▼
No! You can manage everything yourself, or give your receptionist access with specific permissions. The platform is designed to work with or without support staff.
Can I sync MediBook with my existing calendar?
▼
Yes! We support integration with Google Calendar, Outlook, and other popular calendar systems. Your schedule stays in sync automatically.
Is patient data secure on MediBook?
▼
Absolutely. We comply with HIPAA standards and use hospital-grade encryption for all data. Your patient information is protected at the highest level.
What happens to patient data if I cancel my subscription?
▼
Your data is yours. If you cancel, we'll provide you with a complete export of all patient records within 30 days. You can also migrate to another system anytime.
How much commission does MediBook take from consultations?
▼
MediBook doesn't take any commission on consultation fees. You keep 100% of what patients pay. We only charge a monthly subscription for the platform.
Can I customize my clinic's page on MediBook?
▼
Yes! You can fully customize your profile with your clinic logo, photos, biography, specialties, and services. Enterprise plans support full white-label customization.
